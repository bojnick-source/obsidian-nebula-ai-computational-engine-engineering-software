## Purpose

Provides plasma physics analysis and propulsion design guidance for FORGE projects
involving plasma-based systems. Covers:

- Electric propulsion (Hall effect thrusters, gridded ion engines, arcjets, PPTs)
- Plasma diagnostic methods (Langmuir probe, optical emission spectroscopy,
  Faraday cup, retarding potential analyser)
- Discharge chamber and channel geometry
- Propellant selection (Xe, Kr, Ar, I2, water) and feed system requirements
- Plume interaction with spacecraft surfaces (erosion, contamination, torque)
- RF plasma (ICP, CCP) for material processing or propulsion
- Thermal and electrical load on thruster components

## Step 1 — Propulsion Performance Requirements

Derive thruster requirements from mission parameters:

```python
import math

def compute_propellant_mass(m_dry_kg, delta_v_m_s, Isp_s, g0=9.81):
    """Tsiolkovsky rocket equation."""
    m_prop = m_dry_kg * (math.exp(delta_v_m_s / (Isp_s * g0)) - 1)
    return m_prop

def compute_thrust(power_W, eta_thrust, Isp_s, g0=9.81):
    """Thrust from input power, thrust efficiency, and Isp."""
    F_N = (2 * eta_thrust * power_W) / (Isp_s * g0)
    return F_N
```

State propulsion type recommendation with rationale (e.g., Hall thruster for
medium power 100–3000 W, moderate Isp 1500–2500 s).

## Step 2 — Thruster Sizing

For the selected propulsion type, compute key design parameters:

| Parameter | Symbol | Value | Source / Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Specific impulse | Isp (s) | 1800 | Hall thruster nominal 200 V discharge |
| Thrust efficiency | η | 0.55 | Literature; 200 V Xe Hall thruster |
| Mass flow rate | ṁ (mg/s) | 4.2 | F / (Isp × g0) |
| Discharge voltage | Vd (V) | 200 | Design choice |
| Discharge current | Id (A) | 5.0 | P_thrust / Vd |
| Magnetic field | B (mT) | 15 | Channel exit, Hall thruster nominal |
| Channel mean diameter | D (mm) | 35 | From power scaling laws |
| Channel width | h (mm) | 8 | D × 0.23 (nominal ratio) |

Cite scaling law or experimental data source for each parameter.

---

## Step 3 — Plume Interaction

Assess plume effects on spacecraft:

- **Backflow sputtering** on solar panels and optics
  - Estimate CEX ion density at panel distance
  - Compute sputter yield for panel material (Si, glass, ITO)
- **Contamination** on thermal radiators and optical surfaces
- **Torque** from plume asymmetry (if off-center mounting)
- **RF interference** with communication antennas

---

## Step 4 — Propellant Feed System

Specify feed system for the selected propellant:

- Tank pressure and volume (xenon: high-pressure tank, 150–200 bar)
- Pressure regulation to thruster inlet (typically 2–5 bar)
- Flow control (proportional valve or sonic orifice)
- Cathode propellant fraction (typically 5–10% of total flow)
- Leak requirements (He leak rate < 10⁻⁶ scc/s)

---

## Step 5 — Lifetime and Erosion

For the design lifetime:

- Channel erosion rate (mm/kh) from material + ion energy
- Grid erosion (for gridded ion engines) — pits-and-grooves failure mode
- Cathode emitter depletion rate
- Compute total impulse margin (design life × margin factor ≥ 1.25)
